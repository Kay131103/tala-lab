WHAT IS A TALA?

A tala is a rhythmic cycle built from three angas: the laghu (a clap followed by finger counts — its length is the jaathi), the drutham (a wave + wave, 2 aksharas), and the anudhrutham (a single tap). 7 families × 5 jaathis = the 35 talas of the carnatic system.

THE MOST-FED TALA

Adi = Chaturashra jaathi Triputa: laghu(4) + drutham(2) + drutham(2) = 8 aksharas — the 4+2+2 you feel in most krithis. It is the first tala every student learns.
